**Consolidated (2026-11-18):**
- Division visibility and navigation order moved to SL-040; this item is the home page presentation.
- It absorbs the home entry point to World Cup fantasy from SL-094.
- It delivers the first usable, configurable version of the home video and news carousel. SL-093 then redesigns them (chaptered HLS presentation, parallax and motion) in Phase 9.

**Issue:**
- `LeagueLanding.jsx` hard-codes three division entries, clip identifiers, poster URLs and carousel ordering in source.
- The home news is a static grid.
- The World Cup page is reached through general fantasy navigation instead of home content.

**Required outcome:**
- **Home configuration:** store ordered home entries per division, with their visibility on the home page and current-season status. Only the newest or current season gets the lead video slot; older entries stay available below or can be hidden.
- **Video configuration:** dictators configure each entry's video and poster through the storage system from Phase 3 (SL-071, SL-073), with no production R2 URL in source. Resolve D-07 for the authoring rules this item needs: chapters or segments, start timestamps, mobile behavior.
- **News carousel:** dictators choose and order the news items shown in a sliding carousel. Resolve D-11 for which sections follow the current division.
- **World Cup entry:** a configurable home entry links to the separate World Cup page.
- **Port and audit:** port `LeagueLanding.jsx` and the home page onto this configuration and remove the hard-coded entries. Record changes in the SL-027 history.

**Completion checks:**
- Changes are reversible and never delete historical data.
- Home order, lead video and carousel follow configuration after cache refresh.
- No production media URL, division ordering or clip id remains hard-coded (rule R8).
- The World Cup page is reachable from home content.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator sets the home division order, lead video and carousel from the dictator dashboard, and the home page reflects it. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 (SL-093) only redesigns it.
