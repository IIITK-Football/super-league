**Issue:** Division order and visibility are currently hard-coded; users need the dictator to temporarily hide a competition and choose homepage priority.

**Required outcome:** Store ordered homepage entries and visibility/current-season status. Only the newest/current season receives the lead video slot; older entries remain available below or can be hidden by dictator settings.

**Completion checks:** Changes are reversible, do not delete historical data, and affect public navigation/home consistently after cache refresh.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator sets division visibility and homepage order from the dictator dashboard, and the home page reflects it. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
