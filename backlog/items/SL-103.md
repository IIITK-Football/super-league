**Status:** Not started. Scheduled after the division/season league unification (SL-091); do not start earlier.

**Issue:** Sign-in currently relies on a fixed college domain and Supabase's OAuth redirect flow. A smoother Google flow and dictator-controlled access rules are wanted once the unified division experience exists.

**Required outcome:** Add Google's official Sign in button plus One Tap using Supabase ID-token sign-in, with `iiitkottayam.ac.in` as the account-selection hint; keep the button available when One Tap is suppressed. Add dictator controls for allowed college domains and, optionally, approved individual email addresses. Enforce the configured rules on the trusted side (Auth-level checks and API), alongside frontend messaging. Preserve existing users, sessions, and account data. Keep OAuth secrets out of Git.

**Constraints:** Test Google sign-in only against local or isolated non-production Auth; a Git `develop` branch is not an isolated Auth environment, and production is never a test target.

**Completion checks:** Domain and approved-email rules are editable only by a dictator and enforced server-side; button and One Tap both work; rejected accounts see a clear message; no secrets in the repository; existing accounts keep their user IDs.
