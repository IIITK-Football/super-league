**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** moved from Phase 4 into Phase 2 so the identity work is complete before the Workers port. The identity and roster model and the dictator player-management fix are SL-021. The division-specific supported-team and nickname preferences moved to SL-043 (the division fantasy profile).

**Issue:** A user cannot own their player identity. Nothing links a player to an account; the only tie-ins are email strings. A player cannot edit their own card, portrait or attributes.

**Required outcome:**
- **Link field:** add a nullable, unique `players.user_id` referencing `auth.users(id)` with `ON DELETE SET NULL`. Never use email as a foreign key.
- **Linking rules, enforced in the database on every path (Data API, RPC, API, service role):**
  - the account must be a verified Auth account;
  - one player identity per account;
  - only a dictator can link or unlink, so no user can claim a player;
  - unlinking clears only the association and preserves identity, rosters, statistics and history;
  - unlinked players stay fully supported.
- **Audit:** link and unlink are recorded in the SL-027 history.
- **Dictator dashboard actions:** link (select a player identity and a verified account), unlink, and see link status.
- **Bulk email-to-account import:** keyed by stable player ids and separate from schema migrations. It runs as a dry run first, reports missing, duplicate and ambiguous matches for human resolution, and never auto-resolves ambiguous names.
- **Self-service:** a linked player edits their own permitted profile and FUT fields (portrait, attributes and play style, plus other fields the SL-003 matrix allows) on `/profile`. Protected fields (name, roll number, email, link, roster) stay dictator-only.

**Completion checks:**
- Linking requires a verified account and refuses a second player for the same account.
- No non-dictator can link, unlink or claim on any path.
- Unlinking does not delete or reset player data.
- A player can update only their permitted self-owned fields and only on their own identity.
- Link and unlink appear in the audit history.
- The bulk import never resolves an ambiguous name.
- One identity appears consistently in rosters, match events, score leaders and fantasy.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a player edits their own permitted profile/FUT fields from the existing profile page, and a dictator links and unlinks a player identity and an account from the dictator dashboard. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
