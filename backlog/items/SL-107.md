## Assignment

**Entry point.** An assigned captain opens their team's season registration. A dictator opens submitted team requests.

### Flow and screen sketch

- Assigned captain → own team → season Team registration → review team and submit request → status.
- Dictator → season registrations → review the requested team entry and assignment changes → explicit approval and audit result.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. Captain submits for their assigned team only. Submission is a request and leaves team entries, assignments, player records and roster memberships intact.
2. On explicit approval, the dictator reviews the target team/season and intended captain. Apply the confirmed team-entry and assignment changes atomically using existing uniqueness and capability rules. Retry leaves one result. Audit the actor and changes.
3. Wrong-team and unauthorized approval attempts are refused. A stale assignment requires review rather than silently transferring authority. Individual player applications and roster placement stay on their own paths.
