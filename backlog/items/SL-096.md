**Issue:** The shared division frontend needs a welcoming registration flow that recognizes returning players and applies first-year requirements without repeating existing profile questions.

**Required outcome:** Implement the player-facing flow defined in SL-045: identify the signed-in player, welcome returning players, display saved details for confirmation/correction, collect only missing or division/season-specific answers, and apply the configured first-year rule and validation. Show registration open/closed state, remaining capacity or waitlist availability, and the applicable published rules; capture acknowledgment of that exact rules version when required. Provide clear draft, submission, edit, waitlist, and confirmation states across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

**Completion checks:** New and returning player paths follow the same configured form; known name/roll number are not repeatedly requested; first-year hostel fields appear only when the roll-number rule matches; validation errors are understandable and consistent with server validation; capacity/deadlines prevent invalid submissions and full registrations enter the waitlist flow; accepted rules version is recorded; repeat registration preserves one player identity and creates the correct division/season registration record.

**Scope note (rule R10):** the first usable flows come from their owning items (SL-045). This item consolidates, visually redesigns and refines those flows; it does not make them usable for the first time. Any capability still lacking a usable flow is a defect of its owning item.
