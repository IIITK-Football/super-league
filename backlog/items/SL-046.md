**Issue:** Dictators need to review registrations and share operational data, while migrating player identities and correcting matches can create duplicates or inconsistencies.

**Required outcome:** Provide dictator-authorized exports for registrations, team/player participation, match records, and season statistics in a practical format such as CSV. Add integrity reports for incomplete registrations, duplicate account/player links, missing relationships, and score/event/statistic mismatches. Exports must respect role permissions and exclude secrets and unrelated private account data.

**Completion checks:** Exported rows reconcile with filtered dashboard totals; integrity findings identify affected records and explain the issue; exports are scoped to the selected division/season; tests confirm non-dictators cannot access exports or other players' private registration answers.
