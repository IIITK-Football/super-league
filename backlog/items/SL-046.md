## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator picks a division and season in Registration or data exports.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Filter and export, reconcile totals, triage integrity findings, and verify unauthorized accounts cannot export or see any private answers.

### Screen sketch

Dictator → selected season → Exports → filters, matching row count, download and integrity findings with affected records.

**Issue:** Dictators need to review registrations and share operational data, while migrating player identities and correcting matches can create duplicates or inconsistencies.

**Required outcome:** Provide dictator-authorized exports for registrations, team/player participation, match records, and season statistics in a practical format such as CSV. Add integrity reports for incomplete registrations, duplicate account/player links, missing relationships, and score/event/statistic mismatches. Exports must respect role permissions and exclude secrets and unrelated private account data.

**Completion checks:** Exported rows reconcile with filtered dashboard totals; integrity findings identify affected records and explain the issue; exports are scoped to the selected division/season; tests confirm non-dictators cannot access exports or other players' private registration answers.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator runs an export and reads its integrity report from the dictator dashboard. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging.

**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** integrity reports read the canonical models from SL-021, SL-042, SL-022 and SL-045. Exports go through the Worker API and are recorded in the SL-027 history.
