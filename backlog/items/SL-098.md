## Assignment

**Entry point.** Visitor opens the global navigation at phone, tablet portrait and landscape, and desktop sizes.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Reproduce the missing navigation, verify touch and keyboard operation at breakpoints, and check consistent loading, empty, error and stale states.

### Screen sketch

Global navigation → persistent top-level links at desktop widths and an operable menu at compact widths. Check phone, tablet portrait/landscape and desktop with keyboard and touch.

**Issue:** The user reports that the navbar disappears at tablet aspect ratios. Current home loading copy is “Aggregating Live Data...” and loading states vary by page.

**Required outcome:** Reproduce and repair tablet navigation at agreed viewport/aspect test sizes; use shared skeleton/loader states that preserve page layout and do not show noisy implementation text. Improve perceived and measured load time through route/data/media loading behavior.

**Completion checks:** Navigation works by keyboard/touch at phone, tablet portrait/landscape, and desktop sizes; no content is covered; loading, empty, error, and stale-data states are consistent and understandable; performance budgets are defined and measured.
