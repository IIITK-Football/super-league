**Status:** Approved by owner 2026-11-16 — `docs/decisions/SL-002-domain-glossary.md` (glossary, ER diagram, division-agnostic domain requirement).


**Issue:** Division, season, edition, and fantasy currently overlap. A reusable model needs stable names and relationships.

**Required outcome:** Treat division as the formal competition identity and season as one run within a division. Keep World Cup fantasy on its own page and domain, sharing account/auth primitives where appropriate without forcing it into division-specific assumptions.

**Completion checks:** One documented domain glossary and data relationship diagram cover divisions, seasons, phases, teams, memberships, matches, results, players, users, predictions, and configuration. Product-facing and technical naming use “division” consistently.
