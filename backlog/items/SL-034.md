## Assignment

**Entry point.** Start from the production and preview route and caller inventory.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Confirm the Worker owns active traffic, rehearse restoration of a compatible pair and retain shared handlers, then retire the old Next deployment and runtime after the rollback window.

**Disposition: partial.** The [Worker route table](../../api/src/routes.ts) mounts the compatibility surface. [Next runtime scripts and dependencies](../../backend/package.json) and the shared handlers are still present. Their presence does not establish whether an obsolete deployment still serves any caller.

**Completion checks:** Verify active API flows through the Worker and explicitly document production and preview routing. Inventory remaining Next-only callers, routes, and deployment assumptions. Exercise rollback to a prior compatible Worker and Pages release, then retire the obsolete Next deployment and runtime dependencies after active-flow parity and the rollback window. Preserve shared handlers until their Worker imports are safe. Any production action remains owner-approved.
