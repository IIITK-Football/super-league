## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator opens Sign-in access. A new account tries email/password or Google sign-up.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Test both approved routes, denied sign-up, grandfathered verified sign-in, denied email change, and audited configuration.

### Screen sketch

Dictator → Sign-in access → allowed domains and individual email addresses → save and view audit history.

**Issue:** Sign-in is restricted by one college domain hard-coded in the `enforce_college_domain` Auth trigger (`iiitkottayam.ac.in`). Dictators cannot allow another domain or an individual guest address.

**Required outcome:**
- Store the allowed college domains and, optionally, approved individual email addresses as dictator-managed configuration.
- Enforce the rules on the trusted side:
  - in the Auth-level check (the `auth.users` insert and email-change triggers read the configuration instead of a constant);
  - in the Worker API.
- Show a clear message to rejected accounts.
- Preserve existing users, sessions and user ids. The current domain stays allowed unless a dictator removes it, and removing a domain preserves existing verified accounts and their sessions. Apply the new rule to sign-ups and email changes.
- Record changes in the SL-027 history.
- Test only against local or isolated non-production Auth. Keep OAuth secrets out of Git.

**Completion checks:**
- Domain and approved-email rules are editable only by a dictator and are enforced server-side for password and Google sign-up.
- A rejected account sees a clear message.
- Existing accounts keep their user ids.
- The default configuration reproduces today's behavior.
- No secret is in the repository.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator manages the allowed domains and emails from the dictator dashboard and sign-up follows them. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging.
