## Assignment

**Entry point.** Open the actual existing home carousel through Pages locally and on staging, using separately authorized staging media preparation.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. Populate isolated staging media through an owner-authorized step, preserving HLS relative paths and object metadata. Use the staged intro bucket and representative existing upload and share-image paths. Record the served Worker and Pages versions and environment.
2. Browse the actual carousel through Pages against the staging video binding. Play a chapter and transition to another; verify each master, variant and segment request, content types, valid and invalid Range responses, and playback continuity. Repeat locally against deterministic fixtures.
3. Unavailable media shows the configured poster fallback. Verify a staging share preview resolves its staging image and that nonproduction media paths do not silently fall back to production.
4. Track a failing playback scenario here until verified. This is the dependency for home media and sharing, while production hostname migration remains [SL-073](SL-073.md).
