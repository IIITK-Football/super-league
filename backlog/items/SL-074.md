## Assignment

**Entry point.** Owner provisions replacements for the bucket-specific credentials after cutover.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Verify upload and screenshot jobs on replacement credentials and validate backup access separately. Revoke the retired credential only after verification.

**Disposition: not started.** [Backup access](../../docs/backups.md) is separately documented. The [screenshot workflow](../../.github/workflows/og-screenshots.yml) has a branch guard and still uses protected R2 secrets. Their bucket scope, credential replacement, verification and revocation need evidence.

**Added (2026-11-20, from SL-100):** the screenshot job gets its own token limited to the screenshot bucket.

**Issue:** Existing Cloudflare access keys must be removed/rotated, but rotating them before new buckets, bindings, and deployment secrets are ready can interrupt uploads and backups.

**Required outcome:** Provision least-privilege new credentials for the new bucket arrangement, update protected deployment secrets, verify the new path, then revoke old credentials. Keep sensitive storage credentials and access policy separate from application media access.

**Completion checks:** No old key remains in active CI/Worker configuration; no key is committed or bundled into the frontend; bucket-specific permissions are least-privilege; backup access is separately verified.

Rotate per bucket only after that bucket's production cutover is verified (SL-073). Revoking or creating production credentials and changing protected deployment secrets are owner-approved actions.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** not applicable (credentials).
