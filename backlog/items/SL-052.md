**Issue:** Yellow and red card events are not part of the current match model or standard match reporting.

**Required outcome:** Record card type, player/team, time, and correction history in the same canonical event stream; display cards as part of match timeline/statistics.

**Completion checks:** Scorer can add/edit/remove a card event; authorized users can view it; invalid team/player associations are rejected; totals do not double count corrections.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a scorer adds, edits and removes cards and sees the match timeline in the scorer workspace, and the public match view shows them. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
