**Issue:** User reports goal/assist statistics are incorrect, and parallel score/event storage currently allows inconsistency.

**Required outcome:** Define and test goal, own-goal, assist, team, standings, leaderboard, prediction grading, and fantasy-point rules. Include historical recalculation and idempotent regrading behavior.

**Completion checks:** Canonical fixtures have known expected standings and player totals; repeated grading does not duplicate points; corrections cause a safe, deterministic recalculation; all public leaderboards are season/division scoped.
