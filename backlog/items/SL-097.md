## Assignment

**Entry point.** Visitor opens a live match and later its result on the redesigned view.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Show goals, corrected attribution, lineups, and correct match and competition winner states. A celebration plays once for its event and respects reduced motion.

**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** the match functions already work through their owners:
- events, corrections and cards (SL-022);
- the phone-first, offline scorer workspace (SL-050, which also took the scorer-view duty this item used to carry);
- lineups (SL-053);
- brackets (SL-054).

This item redesigns the match view and adds the celebrations.

### Screen sketch

Live match → score/status, event timeline and lineups → completed result and winner treatment. Verify both ordinary and reduced-motion presentation.

**Issue:** The working match view shows events and lineups in the existing page patterns. The user-requested goal and trophy animations do not exist.

**Required outcome:**
- Present validated goal, assist, own-goal and card events and the starting lineup/formation in a designed match view, with team colours and the SL-095 player cards.
- Add restrained goal and trophy/winner animations. Motion is optional and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Redesign the scorer workspace's visuals on the SL-090 design system without changing its offline and sync behavior.

**Completion checks:**
- Live viewers see event updates and corrections clearly.
- Goal and assist names can update after the score.
- Match and division winner states show an appropriate animation once, with no repeated or misleading celebration.
- The scorer workspace's SL-050 tests still pass.

**Scope note (rule R10):** this item consolidates, visually redesigns and refines flows that already work. It does not make anything usable for the first time. Any capability still lacking a usable flow is a defect of its owning item.
