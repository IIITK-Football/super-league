**Issue:** The match view does not yet expose the full event/lineup experience, correction workflow, or user-requested goal and trophy animations.

**Required outcome:** Render validated goal/assist/own-goal/card events and starting lineup/formation in match views, use team colors and FUT cards, and add restrained goal and trophy/winner animations. Keep motion optional/respect reduced-motion preferences. Deliver the scorer's phone-first match control view from SL-050 with readable score/timer/team context, a clear event timeline, large touch targets, and persistent offline queue/sync/conflict indicators.

**Completion checks:** Live viewers see event updates and corrections clearly; goal/assist names can update after the score; match and division winner states show an appropriate animation once, with no repeated or misleading celebration; scorers can record and correct events on a phone during connectivity loss, then review and resolve sync conflicts safely.
