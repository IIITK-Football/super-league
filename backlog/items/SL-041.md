**Issue:** Existing established teams and Freshers' registration/team-member path have different structures and management flows.

**Required outcome:** Bring existing team management and Freshers' registration/team builder into one model while preserving useful team registration and captain workflows. Captains may submit team details and, where enabled, lineups using players already created by a dictator. Only dictators may create, remove, rename, or otherwise manage player records. Scope captain access to their assigned team and season; allow dictator assignment/override and configurable registration requirements.

**Completion checks:** All divisions can use registration, team details, team branding, and captain assignment; player record changes are dictator-only; legacy Freshers-only tables are no longer runtime dependencies; existing data maps without losing people or team history.
