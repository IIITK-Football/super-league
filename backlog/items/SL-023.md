**Issue:** There is no general stage model that represents league-only, knockout-only, or combined competitions. Existing brackets rely on specialized logic and/or match ordering.

**Required outcome:** Define configurable stages and advancement rules, a league-to-knockout transition, editable mapping of real matches to bracket slots, seeding/tie-break rules, and penalty outcomes. Keep phase progression independent from the order rows were inserted.

**Completion checks:** The data model represents league only, knockout only, and combined formats; a dictator can map/re-map a scheduled match to a bracket position; standings/advancement can be recomputed and audited.
