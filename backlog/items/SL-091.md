## Assignment

**Entry point.** Visitor opens existing and newly configured divisions on phone, tablet and desktop.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Use the same core experience across layouts. Exercise configured navigation, league/bracket views and registration with existing data.

**Consolidated (2026-11-18):**
- By Phase 9 the earlier division releases already work in the existing frontend through its owning item:
  - seasons and season URLs (SL-020);
  - rosters and player history (SL-021);
  - configuration, sections and navigation (SL-040);
  - fantasy (SL-043);
  - rules (SL-044);
  - registration (SL-045);
  - stages and the league/knockout switch (SL-054).
- This item absorbs the registration-flow redesign from SL-096.
- The `freshers_fixtures` cleanup is already done (SL-020).

### Screen sketch

Public division → current season or season picker → section navigation, league/bracket view, and rules and registration entry points. Use these same entry points for existing divisions and a data-created division.

**Issue:** The working division pages still carry the legacy per-division page structure and visual inconsistencies: `/super-league`, `/wsl` and `/freshers` look and behave differently. The registration flow is functional but not designed.

**Required outcome:**
- Present one designed division experience driven by the selected division and season, with the sections from dictator configuration. Use the SL-090 design system.
- Combine the Freshers team builder and captain tools with the strongest current WSL and Super League match, standings and fantasy presentation.
- Preserve shareable division/season URLs and historical season selection.
- Redesign the registration flow (welcome back, confirmation, waitlist and confirmation states) across mobile, tablet and desktop, without changing its rules.

**Completion checks:**
- All divisions use the same designed core experience.
- Feature variation comes only from validated configuration.
- Adding a season needs no new route.
- No division's existing capability is lost.
- The registration flow keeps every SL-045 behavior.

**Scope note (rule R10):** this item consolidates, visually redesigns and refines flows that already work. It does not make anything usable for the first time. Any capability still lacking a usable flow is a defect of its owning item.
