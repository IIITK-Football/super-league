**Issue:** There is no persisted lineup model or formation-position rendering. The user wants lineups submitted 30 minutes before a match, with team color and player FUT visible.

**Required outcome:** Support 5-a-side (`1-2-2`), 9-a-side (`1-3-3-2`), and 11-a-side (`1-4-3-3`) with the goalkeeper counted as the first position. Let a captain or dictator assign players already on the team to named starting positions and submit/edit the lineup in the 30 minutes before kickoff, subject to a documented lock policy. This capability does not allow a captain to create, remove, or rename player records.

**Completion checks:** Exactly the correct number of starting slots is available for each format; no player occupies two slots; submission/lock timing is explicit; scorer does not gain roster ownership from match access; captain lineup access cannot mutate the player list; public match views can render positions, team colors, and FUT player cards.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a captain or dictator enters a lineup with starting positions, and the match view shows it. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.

**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** lineups select roster memberships (SL-021) of a team entry, and captain access is the SL-024 assignment. The public match view renders positions, team colours and FUT cards in the existing page; SL-097 redesigns it in Phase 9.
