## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator configures registration from the selected season. A signed-in applicant opens that season's Registration section.

### Flow and screen sketch

- Dictator → season Registration settings → dates, capacity, questions, eligibility rules and acknowledgment → Applications tabs for review, waitlist and confirmed, with private identity requests.
- Applicant → season Registration → sign in and verify → confirm identity or select previous-player/first-time path → requirements → rules acknowledgment → review and submit → status and permitted edits.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. A verified player with a linked record confirms saved identity details without retyping them. A legal name or roll-number correction request goes privately to the dictator. Eligibility uses verified details until the correction is approved. Reuse the identity across divisions and seasons.
2. When verification uniquely matches a legacy email, use the existing linked record. Otherwise an applicant who has played before supplies prior season and team privately and can submit. Keep their original submission time while a dictator resolves the match. Require linking to the existing identity before confirmation. Keep ambiguous matches for review without creating a duplicate.
3. A verified first-time applicant gets one linked player record on successful submission. System creation is scoped to that caller's application and runs atomically with it using a retry-safe transaction. A detected existing candidate routes to review. The application creates no role grant or roster place. Existing authorized link and roster paths still apply.
4. Configure first-year questions from a season roll-number prefix and collect hostel details only when applicable. Enforce required fields and safe configurable pattern validation in the UI and server. Store unanswered optional fields as null. Keep personal answers private and preserve historical submissions if the form changes.
5. While registration is open, an edit updates the same application and retains its original submission time and place. Keep one application per player/account per season, including while a link resolution is pending. Record changes. Closing prevents further applicant edits and submissions.
6. Everyone enters Pending review. A dictator confirms the oldest eligible applicant within capacity. Eligible entries in excess of capacity go on the waitlist in order. A vacancy does not promote another applicant automatically; the dictator confirms the next eligible waitlisted entrant first. Enforce capacity and ordering under concurrent reviews.
7. If rules are republished before initial submission, keep draft answers and prompt for the latest published version. Existing submitted applications preserve their acknowledgment, including when other fields are edited. The applicant can view their accepted version and status; other applicants and captains cannot view private answers.

Team registration is [SL-107](SL-107.md). Exports and integrity reporting are [SL-046](SL-046.md).
