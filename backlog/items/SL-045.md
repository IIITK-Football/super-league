**Issue:** Division registrations currently rely on external forms and can repeatedly ask returning players for profile details they have already supplied. First-year requirements also depend on institution-specific roll-number rules.

**Required outcome:** Provide native, dictator-configurable registration forms scoped to a division and season. Link each response to the signed-in Supabase account and a reusable player identity. For returning players, welcome them back, show previously saved details for confirmation or correction, and ask only for details missing from the player profile or newly required for this division/season. Do not ask again for stored name or roll number unless the player needs to correct it. Keep each registration distinct so the same player can register in multiple divisions or seasons without duplicating their identity.

Dictators configure first-year detection for the relevant season using a roll-number prefix or equivalent rule (for example, prefix `2026`), plus the required first-year fields. If the submitted roll number matches that rule, collect and store hostel name and any other configured first-year details; otherwise do not show those questions. Provide configurable per-field validation, including regex patterns, required/optional state, clear validation messages, and both client-side and server-side enforcement. Regex rules must be validated safely and must not permit pathological expressions to stall requests. Dictators can set registration open/close dates and capacity, see draft/completed counts, and operate a waitlist when full. Where rules acknowledgment is required, store the exact published rules version accepted with the registration.

**Legacy cleanup:** `freshers_fixtures` is absent from hosted Supabase (owner-verified); any registration/fixture flow here must use the canonical model and not depend on that table.

**Completion checks:** A returning signed-in player sees their saved identity details and can confirm them without re-entering known fields; missing or newly required details are collected; first-year questions follow the dictator-configured roll-number rule; phone and other fields honor configured regex validation on client and server; each submitted registration is associated with its player, account, division, and season; unanswered optional fields remain null; duplicate submissions are handled by an explicit edit/resubmit policy; registration windows, capacity, waitlist, and completion status are visible to dictators; a response records the published rules version accepted; dictators can revise form configuration without rewriting historical responses.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator configures a registration form and its window, and a signed-in player or captain completes it, in the existing frontend. The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
