## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator opens season fantasy settings. A signed-in visitor opens division Fantasy.

### Flow and screen sketch

- Dictator → season → Fantasy settings → enabled, supported prediction types and point configuration.
- Visitor → division Fantasy → current-season fixture cards and leaderboard. Signed-in users set a division nickname and supported team, and can open their saved picks.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. Configure scoreline and win/draw/loss prediction types and their points independently per season. A signed-in user submits and updates supported predictions until the earlier of scheduled kickoff and live status. An ordinary delay does not reopen locked picks.
2. Verify prediction points and rankings remain season-scoped after grading. The division Fantasy entry opens only its visible current season and does not offer a historical-season picker. Preserve division flair data when adding nickname.
3. Disable fantasy midseason. Hide its main navigation entry and stop new or changed submissions. Existing participants can open their own saved picks and the leaderboard directly in read-only mode. Re-enabling does not unlock matches that already passed the deadline.
4. Remove the legacy Fantasy switcher and World Cup navigation entry. Keep the separate archive path at /wc under [D-02](../../docs/decisions/D-02-world-cup-separation.md).
