**Issue:** World Cup data is in separate tables, yet profile/nav/fantasy components couple it to general season fantasy. Current division fantasy capability is not uniformly configurable across competitions.

**Required outcome:** Define a reusable fantasy engine/configuration for supported division predictions and scoring, while moving World Cup predictions, flair, scoring, and leaderboard to an independent competition page/domain. Keep World Cup entry reachable from configurable home content.

**Completion checks:** Each competition's fantasy can be switched on/off and configured independently; no World Cup field is required to use normal campus division fantasy; World Cup prediction history and points are preserved and independently graded.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until a dictator turns division fantasy on or off and configures it per season, and a user makes and sees predictions for that season, in the existing pages (World Cup fantasy stays on its own page). The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
