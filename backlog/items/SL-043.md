**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** this item absorbs:
- the functional part of SL-094: World Cup is no longer required by onboarding, profile or division navigation, and its data is preserved;
- the division-specific supported-team and nickname preferences, moved from SL-042.

The home entry link to World Cup is SL-060.

**Issue:**
- World Cup data is in separate tables, yet onboarding forces a World Cup flair (`ProtectedRoute` → `Onboarding`).
- The profile and fantasy navigation couple World Cup to division fantasy.
- Division fantasy cannot be switched on or configured per season.

**Required outcome:**
- **Division fantasy:** a reusable engine and configuration per season (on/off, prediction types, scoring parameters) on the Worker API. Points and leaderboards stay season-scoped, as SL-020 delivered.
- **Fantasy profile:** each division has its own supported team and nickname. The existing `division_flairs` table is extended or replaced, and no data is lost.
- **World Cup:**
  - Remove the World Cup requirement from onboarding and from the division profile and navigation.
  - World Cup predictions, flair, scoring, awards, knockout predictions and leaderboard stay on their own page and domain, with history and points preserved and graded independently.
  - World Cup changes stay minimal and isolated (CLAUDE.md), with no change to its page's user-visible behavior beyond its entry point.

**Completion checks:**
- Each season's fantasy can be switched on or off and configured independently.
- No World Cup field is required to use campus division fantasy.
- A new account can finish onboarding without a World Cup choice.
- World Cup prediction history and points are preserved and independently graded.
- Nickname and supported team are division-scoped.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until all of the following work in the existing pages (World Cup fantasy stays on its own page):
- a dictator turns division fantasy on or off and configures it per season;
- a user sets their division nickname and supported team, and makes and sees predictions for that season;
- a new user onboards without World Cup fields.

The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
