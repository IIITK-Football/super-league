**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** this item absorbs the division-specific supported-team and nickname preferences, moved from SL-042. D-02 freezes World Cup; preserve its current behavior and keep all implementation here focused on division fantasy.

The home entry link to World Cup is SL-060.

**Issue:**
- Division fantasy cannot be switched on or configured per season.

**Required outcome:**
- **Division fantasy:** a reusable engine and configuration per season (on/off, prediction types, scoring parameters) on the Worker API. The user-facing fantasy view selects and shows only the selected division's current season; do not add a historical-season picker. Points and leaderboards stay season-scoped, as SL-020 delivered.
- **Fantasy profile:** each division has its own supported team and nickname. The existing `division_flairs` table is extended or replaced, and no data is lost.
- **World Cup:** no implementation changes. Keep existing data and behavior intact at `/wc`, separate from division fantasy (D-02). A home-carousel link to `/wc` may be handled later as ordinary home content.

**Completion checks:**
- Each season's fantasy can be switched on or off and configured independently.
- The fantasy view for a division opens on that division's current season and does not offer historical seasons.
- World Cup behavior and data are unchanged by the division-fantasy work.
- Nickname and supported team are division-scoped.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until all of the following work in the existing pages (World Cup fantasy stays on its own page):
- a dictator turns division fantasy on or off and configures it per season;
- a user sets their division nickname and supported team, and makes and sees predictions for that season.

The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 only consolidates and refines it.
