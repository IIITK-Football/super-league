**Issue:** The score needs to update immediately even when scorer/assist identity is unknown; current duplicate JSON/event representations make later correction risky.

**Required outcome:** Support a goal with known or unknown scorer and assist, then let a scorer edit attribution, minute, own-goal flag, or event existence later. Make the match score reflect the approved event/score workflow.

**Completion checks:** Goal count and scoreline cannot silently disagree; changing an attribution updates goals/assists and fantasy calculations correctly; removed or corrected events are auditable.
