## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator issues a partner key. A consumer calls the versioned feed.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Verify scoped visibility, stable fields, credential rejection/revocation, 304, per-key 429 with Retry-After, and propagation of a corrected result.

### Flow and screen sketch

- Dictator → partner feed access → create a named key, copy its value once, or revoke it. Keep credential values out of logs. Show the partner label and status.
- Partner → authenticated versioned feed request → visible seasons, fixtures, scores/results and standings with stable IDs and timestamps.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. A valid partner key reads the same dataset across all visible public seasons, including historical ones. Hidden and archived divisions/seasons are excluded. Missing, invalid and revoked keys cannot get a response from a shared cache.
2. The published v1 contract includes divisions, seasons, fixtures, results and standings. Individual events, player names and private or account fields are outside v1. Verify response schema and read-only behavior.
3. A conditional request with a valid key returns 304 when the public representation is unchanged. A corrected result appears after its documented cache interval. Enforce per-key limits with 429 and Retry-After.
4. Record issuance, revocation and contract/version maintenance under [D-09](../../docs/decisions/D-09-external-results.md). Walk through the actual dictator screen and an authorized partner request locally and on staging.
