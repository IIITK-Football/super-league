**Reframed (2026-11-20, owner):** Super League provides fixture and result data for other websites to consume. It does not import from Be.tech or any other fixture provider. The inbound-adapter design is withdrawn; D-09 now covers the outbound feed.

**Issue:** No outbound feed exists. Other sites would have to scrape pages or call routes shaped for the current UI, which have no stability, caching or rate-limit guarantees.

**Required outcome:** A versioned, read-only feed (`/api/v1/feed/...`) of divisions, seasons, fixtures, results, events and standings for public, visible seasons, specified in `docs/decisions/D-09-external-results-proposal.md`.
- **Contract and versioning:** stable ids, documented schema, deprecation policy.
- **Caching:** `ETag`, `Last-Modified`, edge `s-maxage` with stale-while-revalidate, and 304 on conditional requests.
- **Limits:** per-consumer or per-IP rate limits with `429` and `Retry-After`.
- **Access:** as decided in D-09 (anonymous, keyed, or both).
- **Writes:** none. Match and event writes stay in the authorized scorer and dictator flows.

**Completion checks:**
- The feed returns only public, visible seasons and no account data; hidden and archived seasons never appear.
- Responses are schema-validated, stable between versions, and cached and rate-limited as documented.
- A scorer's correction appears in the feed after the cache window; the feed cannot change any match data.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** if keys are chosen in D-09, this item delivers a minimal dictator screen to issue and revoke them in the existing dashboard (real authorized action; loading, empty, success and error states; UI-to-API-to-database test). A fully public feed needs no UI.

**Consolidated (2026-11-18, amended 2026-11-20):** the feed reads the canonical match events (SL-022) through the Worker API and the division, season and stage models (SL-040, SL-054).
