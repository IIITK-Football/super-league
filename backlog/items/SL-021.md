**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** this slice now carries the whole identity-and-roster port. It absorbs the roster, team-entry and Freshers-roster parts of SL-041, the broken dictator player-management flow noted in SL-042, and the player-history data that SL-095 only presents. Account linking and self-editing follow in SL-042. Captain assignment (still `team_registrations.captain_id` until then) is SL-024.

**Issue:** `players` are team-bound (`players.team_id`, `players.team_entry_id`, `unique_team_jersey` on team rather than season). Freshers `team_members` are a second, unlinked person-like table: the seed's Freshers teams hold the same people in both tables. `/api/players` merges the two lists, and `MatchTimeline` cannot resolve member ids. The dictator's player creation is broken (see below). A player has no history across seasons.

**Required outcome:**
- **Identity:** `players` becomes the reusable player identity and keeps its ids, so `goals`, match JSON, predictions, URLs and session links stay valid. Display name, portrait, overall rating, attributes and play style live on the identity (owner decision 2026-11-18).
- **Statistics (owner decision 2026-11-18):** goals and assists are season-specific facts derived from match events, never stored on the identity or the membership.
  - The identity carries one reusable FUT card across seasons; in a season context it is shown with that season's membership and stat line.
  - `players.assists` (a cross-season counter) is retired here, after a dictator-only snapshot for SL-022 to reconcile.
  - Every season stat read (roster, player history, leaderboard, home, legends) goes through one backend source.
- **Season roster:** a `roster_memberships` record places an identity on a team entry for one season, with that season's jersey number and position. Jersey numbers are unique per team entry, not per team. The same identity can appear in several seasons, teams and divisions without duplication. History is preserved when a player changes team or division.
- **Private details:** contact email and other protected identity data move to a private record readable only by dictators (and, once linked in SL-042, by the player). They are never in a public read path. Public display fields and private data have explicit, tested RLS.
- **Data migration (forward-only, guarded, transactional):**
  - Every existing player gets one membership on its 2026 team entry.
  - Each Freshers `team_member` maps to an identity:
    - exactly one same-team, same-season normalized-name match → link to that player;
    - no match → create an identity and a membership;
    - more than one candidate → the migration stops, reports the ambiguous rows and changes nothing (owner decision 2026-11-18; SL-004: never auto-merge silently).
  - Guards verify row counts, ids, goals and membership counts before anything is dropped. The mapping is documented in a decision record.
- **Full port:** move every reader and writer to the identity and membership model:
  - API routes: `/api/players`, `/api/players/:id`, the `/api/leaderboard` Freshers branch, `/api/admin/players` (create, update, delete), `/api/admin/team-registrations` roster writes, `/api/admin/freshers/player-image`, `/api/admin/matches/live` player lookup, `/api/home/dashboard`.
  - Views and RPCs: `season_player_stats` (club from the season's membership, not `players.team_id`), the `captain_update_team_member` RPC, rewritten as a captain roster RPC over memberships with the same SL-003 allow-list and assignment rule.
  - Frontend: `MatchTimeline`, `LiveController`, `Teams`, `PlayerProfile`, `TeamBuilder`, `ManagePlayers`, `ManageRegistrations`.
  - Seed, staging checks and pgTAP.
  - Then retire `team_members`, `players.team_id`, `players.team_entry_id` and `unique_team_jersey`, behind guards that stop unless every record survived.
- **Captains:** may edit only the allow-listed presentation fields of players with a membership on their assigned entry. They never add, remove or rename players, and never touch protected fields.
- **Dictators:** manage identities and memberships.

**Completion checks:**
- The same person appears in multiple seasons and teams with one identity.
- Several unlinked players are valid.
- Every legacy player and Freshers member is accounted for by the documented mapping, and an ambiguous match stops the migration.
- Goals and statistics stay attached after a transfer.
- Public reads expose no private detail.
- Captains cannot create, remove, rename or move players on any path (RPC, API or direct Data API).
- No runtime reader of `team_members`, `players.team_id` or `players.team_entry_id` remains, and those are dropped with every record kept.
- All existing pages (teams, statistics, legends, home, fantasy players, live scoring, team builder) show the same rosters as before for 2026.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** this item is not complete until all of the following work in the existing frontend:
- A dictator uses the existing "Teams & Rosters" dashboard tab with dependent **division → season → team entry** selectors, listing only the entries of the chosen division and season.
- In that tab the dictator adds an existing or new team to the selected season (a `team_entries` row; team identity and branding stay division-wide).
- The dictator sees a team's players only after selecting the team, chooses a player from a dropdown before editing, and creates, edits, moves or removes roster memberships and player identities.
- A captain edits the allow-listed fields of their roster in the team builder.
- A visitor opens a player and sees a minimal history: seasons, teams, goals and assists per season.

The flow follows the current frontend patterns (no redesign), exposes the real authorized action, handles loading, empty, success and error states, and is verified through the real UI, API and database path (allowed and denied cases) locally and on staging. Phase 9 (SL-095) only redesigns it.

**Found during SL-020 (2026-11-19), moved from SL-042:** `backend/app/api/admin/players/route.ts` writes `players.name` and `players.email`, which the `players` table has never had, so creating a player from the dashboard already fails after its image upload. It also does not set the season roster (`players.team_entry_id`). Fix it as part of this port.

**Dictator team-entry workflow (owner request 2026-11-19, moved from SL-041):** the selectors, adding a team to a season and the per-team player dropdown described above. The SL-020 schema already holds team entries. The captain-change part of that request belongs to SL-024.
