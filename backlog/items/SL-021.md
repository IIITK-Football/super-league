**Issue:** `players` are team-bound; Freshers `team_members` are a second person-like table; profile email/nickname/flair fields do not create a persistent footballer identity.

**Required outcome:** Define an independent player/person identity and related participation records for team, division, season, statistics, jersey, position, image, and FUT attributes. Link an identity to Supabase Auth through a nullable, unique `user_id` foreign key to `auth.users(id)` with `ON DELETE SET NULL`; do not use email as a foreign key. Preserve historical appearances when a player changes teams or divisions. Keep unlinked players valid.

**Completion checks:** The same person can appear in multiple seasons/teams without duplicate identity; account linking requires a verified Auth account and cannot be claimed by another user; multiple unlinked players can have `user_id = NULL`; public display fields and private account data have explicit access rules; old player and Freshers roster data have a documented mapping.
