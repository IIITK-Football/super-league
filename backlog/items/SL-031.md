## Assignment

**Entry point.** Open the actual local and staging Pages frontend.

### Acceptance scenario

1. Confirm browser requests reach the Worker through same-origin /api with bearer forwarding, stable errors and cache headers. Restore a compatible Pages and Worker pair and repeat the request.

**Disposition: partial.** [Pages forwards the original request through its API service binding](../../frontend/functions/api/%5B%5Bpath%5D%5D.js). The [forwarding tests](../../frontend/src/lib/__tests__/pagesApiProxy.test.js) check request identity and a missing binding. The [local setup](../../docs/local-development.md) documents the Vite proxy, and the [staging guide](../../docs/worker-staging.md) documents the binding and health endpoint.

**Completion checks:** Record the served version and actual browser-origin requests locally and on staging. Verify bearer forwarding with a protected request, correct missing-session and missing-binding responses, and stable public response, error and cache behavior. Keep the existing `/api` contract. Document rollback by restoring a prior compatible Worker and Pages release and checking the same request through its binding.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** Not applicable. This is routing infrastructure.
