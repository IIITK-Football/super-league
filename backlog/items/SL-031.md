**Issue:** The requested gateway/service-binding design is not present. A browser calling a separate Worker origin would still be cross-origin; service bindings only address worker-to-worker calls.

**Required outcome:** Define external request routing and internal service binding. Choose how the Pages site reaches the gateway: same-origin Pages Function/proxy or a deliberately configured cross-origin endpoint. Keep `/api` behavior or a compatibility adapter until the final frontend migration.

**Completion checks:** Browser requests have a tested same-origin or correct CORS path; worker-to-worker traffic uses internal bindings where intended; public route responses have stable CORS, auth, error, and cache behavior.
