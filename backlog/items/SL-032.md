**Disposition: partial.** The [Worker route table](../../api/src/routes.ts) serves shared handlers. The [compatibility baseline](../../docs/contracts/worker-compatibility.md) inventories 44 operations across 33 mounted paths and direct browser dependencies. [Worker tests](../../api/test) cover representative routes; [SL-025](../../docs/walkthroughs/SL-025.md) records limited staging evidence.

**Remaining work:** Use the baseline as the route-group parity register for public division data, schedules/results, teams/players/standings, account/profile, predictions, role-scoped match and roster operations, editorial, World Cup and media. Record pass/fail evidence per group for contract and authorization tests against local Supabase and existing frontend flows through the Worker/Data API locally and on staging. Polls and `freshers_fixtures` are retired. Keep the World Cup archive on `/wc` and verify retrieval of a saved bracket, including its initial round. Define cutover evidence and restore a prior compatible Worker and Pages release for rollback.

**Home carousel blocker:** Keep the reported failure open. Verify a real HLS master and referenced playlists and segments through `/api/media/*` backed by `R2_VIDEO`. Record manifest and segment responses, content types, and valid and invalid Range behavior, plus actual browser playback across a carousel transition. A health check or standalone manifest response does not satisfy this check. Review route groups independently of the full storage migration. The binding implementation is SL-071, local fixtures are SL-072, and staging bucket setup and production cutover are SL-073. Staging verification of the video path uses the populated staging bucket before production cutover.

**Usable in its own phase (rule R10):** Existing flows need real local and staging evidence for each ported group.
