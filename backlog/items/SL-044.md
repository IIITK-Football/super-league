## Assignment

**Entry point.** Dictator opens the selected season's Rules editor at /dictator. A visitor opens its Rules page.

### Flow and screen sketch

- Dictator → selected season → Rules → block list with add, reorder and visibility controls → preview, publish, and version history.
- Visitor → season Rules → latest published version. Player → own registration → exact accepted version when SL-045 lands.

Use a block list with one selected block editor. Keep Preview and Publish distinct and place the version history alongside draft status. Preview opens the visitor presentation of the draft with a clear preview label.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. Migrate existing rules content and verify it is still readable. Retire the seasons.rules_md reader and field only after migration verification.
2. Assemble titled sections, eligibility callouts, numbered steps, tables and notices with structured fields. Reorder and hide blocks without deleting them. Preview as a visitor would see them.
3. Publish the draft and audit the event. Edit a new draft without changing what visitors see. Publish again and verify the previous version remains immutable and available to the dictator and, through registration, the player who accepted it. Refuse non-dictator writes and other players' private history links.
