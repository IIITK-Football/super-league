**Status:** Not started. Do with or after SL-073 (custom domain for R2), and after the production cutover is stable; do not start earlier.

**Issue:** Google's account chooser and consent screen show the Supabase project hostname (`<ref>.supabase.co`), which users do not recognise as this project.

**Required outcome:** Serve production Supabase Auth from a subdomain of the purchased domain, planned together with the R2 domain from SL-073 (one DNS plan, distinct subdomains, for example `auth.` and a media subdomain), while the website stays on Pages.dev. Add the new callback URL to the production Google OAuth client before switching, keep the old callback working until verified, and update the frontend and backend Supabase URLs through the normal release gates. Local and staging keep their own hosts, Google clients and redirect lists; only production uses the branded domain. Confirm plan and add-on cost first (Supabase custom domains need a paid plan), and consider Google OAuth brand verification as the cheaper interim improvement. Coordinate with SL-103, since ID-token sign-in changes which domain Google displays.

**Completion checks:** Google shows the project domain on production; existing sessions and user IDs are unchanged; the old hostname keeps working until cutover verification; staging and local are unaffected; no secrets in Git.
