**Consolidated (2026-11-18):** the functional pieces already work through their owners:
- the player identity and the minimal player history across seasons (SL-021);
- self-editing of the FUT card and photo, and the linked account (SL-042);
- division-scoped nickname and supported team (SL-043);
- lineups (SL-053).

This item is the reusable card and its presentation.

**Issue:** Selecting a player replaces the team screen with a full PlayerProfile view. The desired component expands in place, and FUT identity should appear wherever player names are displayed.

**Required outcome:**
- Build one reusable player card, used in rosters, match events, lineups, scorer and assist leaders, and other name displays.
- In the team list, expand the first player's card by default, allow only one card open at a time, and collapse or expand by selecting names.
- Show the linked account's nickname and image where permitted.
- Present the player history (seasons, teams, appearances, goals and assists) in the designed card.
- On leaderboards, cards can be revealed while scrolling without obscuring data or harming accessibility.

**Completion checks:**
- Profile behavior is consistent across all division pages.
- Inline expansion preserves the user's team context.
- The account/profile control uses the linked player's image when available.
- Player history preserves season boundaries and team changes.

**Scope note (rule R10):** this item consolidates, visually redesigns and refines flows that already work. It does not make anything usable for the first time. Any capability still lacking a usable flow is a defect of its owning item.

**Shared FUT card and canonical attributes (2026-11-19):**
- **One card component:** `frontend/src/components/players/FutCard.jsx`, extracted from the Clubs player page, renders the card in both places. The Clubs page uses it with the season's position in the corner. The dictator's Teams & Rosters forms use it as a live preview of the unsaved draft, including a picture that is not saved yet, and show the season's jersey and position beside it, never inside it. There is no separate placeholder design.
- **One canonical attribute shape** (`players.attributes`, the reusable identity shared across seasons), enforced by the `players_attributes_canonical` check (`20261130000002`) and mirrored by `frontend/src/lib/playerCard.js`:
  - `stats`: `Pace | Shooting | Passing | Dribbling | Defending | Physicality`, each `{ total: 1..99, ... }`;
  - `bio.preferredFoot`: `Right | Left | Both`;
  - `playStyles`: `[{ name, description, icon_url }]` from `frontend/src/data/playStyles.js`;
  - any other key is kept. Older flat numbers (`{ pace: 59 }`) were converted; values that could not convert are kept under `legacy`.
- **What this item still owns:** the polished card presentation.
  - The designed card visuals and tiers (the current card reuses the existing gold, silver and bronze artwork).
  - All play styles on the card (it shows the first play style's icon).
  - Preferred foot on the card face (the dictator preview shows it in a caption).
  - Inline expansion in team lists, one open at a time; season history; the linked account's image.
  - Using the same component wherever player names appear (rosters, match events, lineups, leaderboards).
