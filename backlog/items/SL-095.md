**Issue:** Selecting a player replaces the team screen with a full PlayerProfile flow; the desired component should expand in place, and FUT identity should appear wherever player names are displayed.

**Required outcome:** Build one reusable player card used in rosters, match events, lineups, scorers/assist leaders, and other name displays. In the team list, expand the first player's card by default, allow only one card open at a time, and collapse/expand by selecting names. Show linked account nickname and image where permitted. Provide a player history view across divisions and seasons, including team participation and season-scoped appearances, goals, assists, and other supported statistics.

**Completion checks:** Profile behavior is consistent across all division pages; inline expansion preserves the user's team context; users can edit their own FUT/photo; the account/profile control uses the linked player's image when available; nickname and supported-team choices are division-fantasy scoped; player history preserves season boundaries and team changes; leaderboard presentation can reveal cards during scrolling without obscuring data or accessibility.

**Scope note (rule R10):** the first usable flows come from their owning items (SL-042). This item consolidates, visually redesigns and refines those flows; it does not make them usable for the first time. Any capability still lacking a usable flow is a defect of its owning item.
