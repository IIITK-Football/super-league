# Plan: player identity, season teams and rosters (full port)

Owner decisions (2026-11-18):
- A full port.
- FUT/card data lives on the identity; jersey and position live on the season membership.
- **Goals and assists are season-specific and never live on the reusable identity**; the identity carries one FUT card across seasons (see "Statistics" below).
- An ambiguous Freshers name match stops the migration and reports.
- For a matched Freshers pair, the team-builder (`team_members`) values win where they are set, and every overwritten value is reported.

## Issue
A player is team-bound and there is a second, unlinked roster:
- `players.team_id` and `players.team_entry_id` hold one team and one season per row, and `unique_team_jersey` is per team, not per season.
- Freshers rosters live in `team_members` through `team_registrations`. They are a different id space with no link to `players`.
- The same person can therefore have two rows (the seed's Freshers teams do), and one person cannot play several seasons without a duplicate identity.
- The dictator cannot create a player (the route writes columns that do not exist).
- Captains have broad direct write access to `players` through RLS.
- There is no player history across seasons.

## Evidence
- **Schema** (`20261106000000_baseline.sql`, `20261123000000_season_rosters_and_fantasy.sql`):
  - `players.team_id` → teams SET NULL; `players.team_entry_id` → team_entries RESTRICT; `unique_team_jersey (team_id, jersey_number)`.
  - `goals.player_id` → players **CASCADE** (deleting a player erases goals); `goals.assist_id` SET NULL.
  - `team_members` (registration_id CASCADE, email, position codes, rating 1–99) has no link to `players`.
- **RLS:**
  - `players` has `INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE … WITH CHECK (true)` for every authenticated user, plus "Admin and Captain all access players", contrary to SL-003.
  - `team_members` is readable by the registration's captain and managed by dictator or legacy admin.
- **Freshers ids leak into JSON:**
  - The live route (`/api/admin/matches/live`) records a Freshers scorer by member id in `matches.scorers/assists/own_goals`. Its `goals` insert then fails the players FK unchecked, so Freshers goals exist only in JSON.
  - The Freshers branch of `/api/leaderboard` counts that JSON.
  - Predictions store player ids as string arrays (`predicted_scorers`, `predicted_assists`), and grading compares them with `matches.scorers`.
  - The JSON elements are plain id strings (live route) or `{player_id, …}` objects (seed).
- **Statistics stored on the identity:**
  - `players.assists` is an integer counter written only by the live route (incremented on a goal with an assist, `route.ts:97-98`; decremented on removal, `:192-194`). The standalone `add_assist` action (`:115-118`) updates only the match JSON, so the counter already disagrees with the other sources.
  - It is read only by the roster list `/api/players` (`route.ts:33,73`).
  - Once one identity spans seasons, the counter is a cross-season total that cannot be attributed to any season.
  - `/api/players/[player_id]` counts **all** of a player's goals and assists (`route.ts:36-44`), under the comment "a player row is one season's roster place, so these are that season's goals". That assumption breaks once the identity is reusable.
  - Already season-scoped (SL-020): `season_player_stats`, the leaderboard (with a registration-season branch counting the match JSON), the home dashboard and legends.
- **Readers and writers to port:**
  - API routes: `/api/players` (merges both tables), `/api/players/[id]` (falls back to `team_members`), the `/api/leaderboard` Freshers branch, `/api/home/dashboard`, `/api/admin/players` (writes nonexistent `players.name`/`email`, no roster), `/api/admin/team-registrations` (upserts members), `/api/admin/freshers/player-image`, `/api/admin/matches/live` (player list and the `players.assists` counter), `/api/admin/teams` (team delete).
  - View: `season_player_stats` (club from `players.team_id`).
  - Frontend: `MatchTimeline`, `LiveController`, `Teams`, `PlayerProfile`, `TeamBuilder` (+ `lib/teamMemberUpdate.js`), `ManagePlayers`, `ManageRegistrations`, `Fantasy`.
  - Tests and fixtures: `captain_update_team_member` (both overloads), the seed, `scripts/staging-checks.mjs` (creates `team_members`), and pgTAP 001, 003 and 006.

## Scope

### Statistics: season-specific, derived, never stored on the identity
1. **No goal or assist total is stored on the identity or on the roster membership.**
   - Goals and assists are facts about matches, and a match belongs to a season, so every statistic is season-scoped by construction.
   - A stored copy on a membership would drift from the events.
2. **The season stat line is derived per (season, player) from match events.**
   - `season_player_stats` stays the one interface (`season_id`, `player_id`, club, goals, assists).
   - Club = the team of the player's membership in that season, falling back to the event's team.
   - Cross-season figures (legends, the career line on a player page) are sums of season lines, labelled as such, and never stored.
3. **The card is identity plus context.** The reusable FUT data comes from the identity. In a season context, the card pairs it with that season's membership (jersey, position) and that season's stat line. The player page shows one line per season and a computed career line.
4. **`players.assists` is retired here:**
   - its reader (`/api/players`) and writer (the live route) are ported;
   - the expand migration snapshots it into `legacy_player_assist_counts (player_id, counter_value, event_assists_at_capture, captured_at)` (dictator-only), and the contract migration drops the column;
   - being cross-season, it is not migrated into any season's numbers;
   - SL-022 reconciles the snapshot and drops it.
5. **One backend source for season stats until SL-022:** `backend/lib/seasonStats.ts` returns a season's stat lines.
   - It applies today's rule: the season's match JSON for a season played with registration rosters (the current leaderboard branch, moved unchanged and keyed by player identity), otherwise `season_player_stats`.
   - The leaderboard, home dashboard top scorer and assister, legends, roster list and player page all use it.
   - The one deliberate change: for a registration season, home and legends now agree with the leaderboard.
6. **Hand-over to SL-022:**
   - It replaces the internals of `season_player_stats` and `seasonStats.ts` with canonical event aggregation, keeping their interface.
   - It folds the Freshers JSON-only goals into events, removing the registration-season branch.
   - It reconciles `goals`, the match JSON and the snapshot.
   - It adds own goals, cards and appearances to the season line. Unknown attribution counts toward the score but toward no player's line.
   - Corrections need no recalculation of stored totals because none exist.

### 1. Expand migration `20261124000000_player_identity_rosters.sql` (forward-only, one transaction)
- **`roster_memberships`:**
  - Columns: `id`, `player_id` → players RESTRICT, `team_entry_id` and `season_id` (a composite FK to `team_entries (id, season_id)`, adding that unique key), `jersey_number` (0–99, nullable), `position` (text), timestamps.
  - Unique `(player_id, season_id)`: one team per season.
  - Unique `(team_entry_id, jersey_number)`.
  - Indexes on `team_entry_id` and `season_id`.
- **`player_private_details`:** `player_id` PK → players CASCADE, `email`, timestamps. RLS: dictator-only. SL-042 adds the linked player's own read.
- **`legacy_roster_map`:** `team_member_id`, `player_id`, `outcome` (`linked` | `created`), and the overwritten-values summary. Dictator-only read; kept as the documented history of the mapping.
- **Backfill, in order, with guards that raise and change nothing:**
  1. Every player with a `team_entry_id` gets a membership on it, carrying its `jersey_number` and `position`. Players without a team keep none (reported, as SL-020 did).
  2. Each `team_member`'s registration must have a `team_id`; otherwise stop and report. Ensure the team's entry exists for the registration's season (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`).
  3. Match each member by normalized name against players with a membership on that entry. Normalization: `lower`, trimmed, collapsed whitespace, `first_name || ' ' || last_name`, else `name`.
     - Exactly one candidate, and no other member claims it → **linked**. Member values win where set (jersey, position, rating, attributes, portrait), and each difference is recorded in the map.
     - No candidate → **created**: a new identity **reusing the member's id** (so no JSON or prediction reference changes), with the name split into first and rest, and a membership.
     - Several candidates, or several members on one player → **stop**, listing the rows.
     - A jersey collision on the entry → stop.
  4. Copy non-empty member emails into `player_private_details`. A conflicting email on a linked pair is reported, and the member's value wins.
  5. For linked members only, rewrite references from the member id to the player id: `matches.scorers/assists/own_goals` (string elements and `{player_id}` objects) and `predictions.predicted_scorers/predicted_assists`.
  6. Guards: the number of JSON elements is unchanged, and no element still holds a member id.
- **Data-preservation guards:**
  - player count before plus `created` = player count after;
  - every member appears in the map exactly once;
  - membership count = players with an entry + mapped members not already rostered;
  - goals unchanged;
  - prediction `points_awarded` sum unchanged.
- **Change `goals.player_id` to `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.** Deleting a player who scored is refused; history is never erased.
- **`season_player_stats`:** the club comes from the player's membership in that season, falling back to the goal's `team_id`. Memberships carry no statistics.
- **`legacy_player_assist_counts`:** a snapshot of `players.assists`, with the event-derived assist count at capture. RLS dictator-only, anon revoked.
- **RLS:**
  - Drop the broad and admin/captain policies on `players`. New: public read; dictator insert/update/delete.
  - `roster_memberships`: public read, mirroring `team_entries`; dictator write.
  - Revoke anon writes on the new tables (default privileges grant them).
- **New RPC `captain_update_roster_player(p_membership_id, p_position, p_jersey_number, p_overall_rating, p_attributes, p_image_url)`:**
  - SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path ''`.
  - Allowed: a dictator, or a caller holding the captain role whose `team_registrations` row (`captain_id = auth.uid()`) has the membership's `team_id` and `season_id` (SL-003 rule 3).
  - Updates only the allow-list: position and jersey on the membership; rating, attributes/play style and portrait on the identity.
  - No name, email, roster or link parameter. Same error texts as today; anon and PUBLIC revoked.

### 2. Port every reader and writer (backend, then frontend)
- **`/api/players`:** one season roster from memberships ⨝ players ⨝ team_entries ⨝ teams, with no merge. The response shape is kept (`id, name, first_name, last_name, position, jersey_number, image_url, assists, team_id, team`); `assists` and a new `goals` field come from that season's stat line (`seasonStats.ts`), not `players.assists`.
- **`/api/players/[id]`:**
  - The identity (the FUT card) and its memberships (division, season, team, jersey, position).
  - One stat line per season (`seasonStats.ts`) and a computed, labelled career line. No all-time count is presented as the card's figure.
  - The `team_members` fallback is removed.
- **`/api/leaderboard`, `/api/home/dashboard` and `/api/legends`:** read season stat lines through `seasonStats.ts`. The registration-season rule moves there unchanged, with the player list from memberships, so the numbers do not change until SL-022 unifies events (except that home and legends now agree with the leaderboard for registration seasons).
- **`/api/admin/players`, rebuilt:**
  - POST creates identity + membership + private email (+ optional portrait through the existing R2 helper, keyed by player id) atomically through a SECURITY INVOKER function.
  - PUT edits identity, membership and private fields.
  - DELETE removes an identity with no memberships, goals or predictions, and is otherwise refused with a clear 409.
  - User-bound client, so RLS decides, plus an explicit dictator check for a clear 403. Legacy admin is refused (no inference).
- **New `/api/admin/roster`:** add an existing identity to an entry, change jersey or position, move within a season, remove a membership (the identity stays).
- **`/api/admin/team-registrations`:** keeps team, entry, registration and captain (SL-024 rewrites them). Member writes go through the roster API.
- **`/api/admin/freshers/player-image`:** authorize by membership on the captain's entry; the key is the player id. Existing URLs are untouched.
- **`/api/admin/matches/live`:** the player list is the season roster. It no longer reads or writes `players.assists`.
- **`/api/admin/teams` DELETE:** behavior unchanged (a team with a roster was already undeletable). The 409 message now explains it.
- **Frontend:**
  - `MatchTimeline`, `LiveController`, `Teams`, `Fantasy` and `PlayerProfile` read the unified ids, and PlayerProfile shows the history.
  - `TeamBuilder` reads the captain's entry roster and calls the new RPC. The email display is removed: private, not a captain field.
  - `teamMemberUpdate.js` becomes `rosterPlayerUpdate.js`.
- **Seed:** players without team columns; memberships (`ON CONFLICT (player_id, season_id) DO NOTHING`, so staging reseeds stay idempotent next to migration-created rows); the Freshers teams have no duplicate people; one player with memberships in two seasons and divisions (the history fixture).
- **Staging checks:** use memberships and the new RPC.

### 3. Dictator "Teams & Rosters" flow (rule R10)
`ManageRegistrations` becomes `ManageRosters`, following the `ManageDivisions` patterns (pure validators, `describeError`, per-row busy state, `role=alert`/`role=status`, loading and empty states per level):
- **Navigation:** division → season → team entry selectors; only that season's entries are listed.
- **Add a team to the season:** pick an existing team of the division (direct `team_entries` insert under RLS) or create one (`/api/admin/teams`).
- **Roster:** shown only after a team is selected.
  - Choose a player from a dropdown before editing: identity, membership and private email.
  - Add a new player, add an existing player (search identities), move within the season, remove from the roster, delete an identity without history.
- **Registration and captain fields:** kept as they are (SL-024).
- **`ManagePlayers` tab:** removed; its job is in this flow.

### 4. Retire (contract migration `20261125000000_retire_team_bound_rosters.sql`, same release)
- Guards (stop unless all hold):
  - every member is in `legacy_roster_map`;
  - every player that had a team has a membership;
  - no JSON or prediction element references a missing player id.
- The snapshot holds one row per player with its counter value.
- Then drop `team_members`, `players.team_id`, `players.team_entry_id`, `players.position`, `players.jersey_number`, `players.assists`, `unique_team_jersey`, both `captain_update_team_member` overloads, and the old players policies' leftovers.
- `team_registrations` stays as the captain link only, until SL-024.

## Rollback
- **Before the contract migration:** application code can be reverted freely; the legacy columns and `team_members` still exist and are untouched by the expand step, apart from the rewritten JSON ids of linked members, which now point to real players.
- **After the contract migration:** fix forward with a new migration. `legacy_roster_map` records every member-to-player decision and overwritten value, so a wrong link can be split back into its own identity: create the player with the member's old id, move the membership, and rewrite the recorded references.
- **UI:** `ManageRosters` is one component and one tab; reverting its commit leaves the data intact.
- **Production:** nothing is applied without the reviewed `main` release, a backup or PITR point, and the dry-run report showing no ambiguous rows.

## Implementation notes (deviations from this plan, 2026-11-18)
- **One write path, enforced in the database.** The plan rebuilt `/api/admin/players` and added `/api/admin/roster` with history checks in the routes. Because the dashboard writes through the Data API under RLS (the `ManageDivisions` pattern), those checks would have been bypassable. History protection moved into the database instead:
  - the `roster_memberships_keep_history` trigger;
  - `goals.assist_id` is RESTRICT, like `goals.player_id`.

  Teams & Rosters writes through the Data API and `create_rostered_player`, and both routes were removed. That leaves no second write path. The portrait upload stays a server route (`/api/admin/freshers/player-image`), now keyed by roster place and player id.
- **Season statistics from the view itself, not `seasonStats.ts`.** The registration-season branch of the leaderboard counted the match JSON only for viewers who could read `team_registrations` (captains and dictators); visitors got the goals table. `season_player_stats` now counts, per match and player, the greater of the goals table and the match JSON. That gives the same numbers for every viewer and keeps Freshers JSON-only goals, with no dependency on `team_registrations` (which SL-024 retires first). Every reader uses the view, and SL-022 replaces its internals.
- **Tabs.** Teams & Rosters (season entries and rosters) is a dictator-only tab. The old Players tab, which wrote columns the table never had, is removed. Team registration and captain assignment stay under "Captains" until SL-024.
- **Staging and verification tooling** moved to the new model:
  - `scripts/staging-checks.mjs` (roster places, the new RPC, private details, no direct captain writes);
  - `scripts/verify-rpc-closed.mjs` (the new function closed; the retired overloads closed or gone);
  - `scripts/sql/verify-captain-rpc.sql` and `scripts/sql/verify-preview.sql`.
- **The rehearsal** is `pnpm db:rehearse-rosters --yes`. The guarded `scripts/local-db.sh reset` gained `--version` and `--no-seed`, and the newer migrations are applied with `psql`. It also runs the production dry-run report on the legacy schema and checks its prediction.
- **Review fixes (2026-11-18, forward migration `20261127000000_roster_safety_fixes.sql`, because `20261124`/`20261125` were already applied on staging):**
  - a captain edits only roster places in a current season, through the RPC and the portrait route; the card fields belong to the shared identity, so an earlier season's assignment must not reach a player who moved on;
  - jersey numbers 0-99 everywhere;
  - `create_team_in_season` and `delete_unplayed_player` make the dictator's two multi-write actions single transactions, with no orphan team and no player removed from a roster but left undeleted;
  - the history trigger also refuses moving a roster place with statistics to another player or season;
  - the dashboard never touches a private email it could not load;
  - the team builder ignores a slower roster answer for an earlier team.
- **Known, accepted:** the contract migration's guard checks `players.team_entry_id`, not `team_id`. A player created between `20261123` and `20261124` with only `team_id` would lose that team. This matters only if the two migrations run separately while the old app is live; production receives them in one release.
