# Walkthrough: SL-021 Player identity, season teams and rosters (full port)

Facts between the generated markers are written by `pnpm work done`; do not edit them. Fill in every other section.

## Summary

Players are now reusable identities with one FUT card, shared across every team and season, plus one roster place per season (jersey number and position).
- **The port:** Freshers members were folded into players with a guarded migration that stops, changing nothing, on any ambiguous match. Team-bound rosters and the old member table were retired once every caller was ported.
- **Statistics:** goals and assists are derived per season from match events and never stored on the identity.
- **Dictators** manage teams and rosters in Teams & Rosters:
  - a Division → Season → Team flow; add or create teams;
  - search existing players by full name, with their latest team and season;
  - create, edit, move and remove players with structured FUT card fields and a live card preview, including the portrait before upload.
- **Captains** edit only their own team's current-season roster in the team builder. Dictators can choose any team there.
- **One canonical card shape** is enforced by the database, and nothing is invented: ratings stay unset until entered.
