# Walkthrough: SL-018 Close the captain_update_team_member exposure

Facts between the generated markers are written by `pnpm work done`; do not edit them. Fill in every other section.

## Summary

Production had a security hole: the seven-argument `captain_update_team_member` function ran with its owner's rights, checked nothing about who was calling, had no pinned search path, and was executable by anonymous callers, so anyone holding the public key could overwrite any team member, including the email. Migration `20261118000000` closes it: anonymous and public callers lose execute on both overloads, a caller must be assigned to the player's team AND hold the captain role (a dictator may update any team; everyone else is refused), captains can no longer change a player's email, and the search path is pinned. The migration refuses to run, changing nothing, while any assigned captain lacks the captain role, and the production audit found none. The team builder no longer sends emails or hides a rejected save, and assigning a captain no longer replaces another role. The fix is applied to production through the approved workflow and verified (see the notes on what was and was not tested there).
